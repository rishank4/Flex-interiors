Methodology & Framework

From Vision to Handover

We replace unpredictability with architectural discipline. Our structured 8-stage workflow guarantees transparent budgeting, milestone accountability, and uncompromising on-site precision.

Methodical Delivery

The 8-Stage Architectural Roadmap

Every milestone represents a formal checkpoint ensuring design intent, budget parameters, and craftsmanship standards are fulfilled.

STAGE 01

Discovery & Site Appraisal

We initiate every project on site, laser-scanning the physical structure, examining daylight exposures, and analyzing structural MEP constraints alongside your lifestyle aspirations.

Site Laser Scan MEP Feasibility Budget Parameters
STAGE 02

Concept & Material Moodboards

Establishing the emotional and textural foundation. We curate physical material boards featuring authentic marbles, solid hardwoods, patinated metals, and architectural fabrics.

Material Trays Color Psychology Spatial Narrative
STAGE 03

Space Planning & Schematics

Translating conceptual volume into precise architectural drawings. We optimize internal traffic paths, furniture clearances, sightlines, and natural light penetrations.

2D CAD Layouts Circulation Studies Demolition Plans
STAGE 04

Photorealistic 3D Visualization

Eliminating guesswork through 4K photorealistic architectural renders. You experience the interplay of morning and evening light, fixture finishes, and bespoke millwork before construction starts.

4K Renderings Daylight Modeling Virtual Walkthrough
STAGE 05

Technical Detailing & BOQ Costing

Comprehensive construction documentation covering joinery shop drawings, reflected ceiling plans, electrical layouts, and an itemized, fixed-price Bill of Quantities (BOQ).

Millwork Shop Drawings Reflected Ceiling Plans Itemized BOQ Contract
STAGE 06

Procurement & Bespoke Millwork

Our workshop fabricates custom architectural cabinetry while our procurement team manages long-lead fixtures, appliances, natural stone slabs, and European textiles.

Artisanal Fabrication Slab Inspection Logistics Tracking
STAGE 07

Turnkey Execution & Supervision

On-site construction led by full-time Flex project directors. We coordinate trade specialists, enforce acoustic standards, and provide weekly digital photo milestones.

Dedicated Site Director Weekly Dashboards Quality Checkpoints
STAGE 08

Snagging, Styling & Handover

The final touch: 100-point quality audit, white-glove deep cleaning, art placement, bespoke dressing, and presentation of the comprehensive studio handover archive.

White-Glove Snagging Art Installation As-Built Manual
Timeline Expectations

Indicative Project Durations

While each architectural commission has unique bespoke requirements, our standardized milestones ensure disciplined scheduling.

RESIDENTIAL FITOUT

12 – 16 Weeks

Luxury apartment or penthouse interior fitout, custom modular kitchen, bespoke wardrobe suites, and lighting integration.

  • Weeks 1–4: Concept, 3D & BOQ
  • Weeks 5–12: Fabrication & Site Prep
  • Weeks 13–16: Install, Styling & Handover
FULL GUT RENOVATION

20 – 28 Weeks

Comprehensive structural renovation of townhouses or estates, including MEP overhauls, structural partitions, and historical approvals.

  • Weeks 1–6: Design, Engineering & Board Permitting
  • Weeks 7–22: Structural Build & Millwork
  • Weeks 23–28: High-End Finishes & Commissioning
COMMERCIAL & RETAIL

10 – 14 Weeks

Corporate headquarters, executive conference suites, and luxury flagship retail delivered on strict commercial opening deadlines.

  • Weeks 1–3: Programming & Spatial CAD
  • Weeks 4–10: Fast-Track Fitout & Acoustics
  • Weeks 11–14: Systems Testing & Turnkey Handover
Clarity & Confidence

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers regarding our contracts, budgeting, timelines, and turnkey commitments.

We work exclusively on guaranteed, itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) agreements. Following the 3D visualization phase, every single material, cubic foot of joinery, light fixture, and labor hour is detailed with fixed costs. Unless you choose to expand scope during the build, the contracted figure is the exact figure you pay—eliminating the notorious "contractor creep."

Absolutely. Meaningful architecture honors history. During the Discovery and Site Appraisal stage, we catalog heritage moldings, original fireplaces, parquet flooring, or meaningful art and heirloom furniture, thoughtfully harmonizing them within the new contemporary spatial layout.

Our in-house architectural team manages the entire regulatory pipeline: drafting alteration agreements, submitting engineering packages, presenting to board review committees, and expediting building department permits. We handle all compliance logistics so you never have to navigate bureaucratic red tape.

In traditional setups, the architect blames the builder for executing improperly, while the builder blames the architect for drafting unbuildable details—leaving the client caught in the middle. With Flex Interiors, one unified entity designs the vision, drafts the shop drawings, manages the site, and takes 100% legal and moral accountability for the outcome.

Our process includes two formal rounds of iterative revisions during the concept and 3D rendering phases. Because we render in photorealistic 4K with physical material trays present, you see and feel the exact result before a single hammer is swung on site.

Every Flex project includes an extensive comprehensive 2-year warranty on all bespoke architectural joinery, finishes, and contractor installations, alongside the manufacturer warranties for all integrated appliances and lighting fixtures. Furthermore, our client concierge remains at your service for any future adjustments.
